# Report Design Guidance

The final report must be rendered to DOCX and PDF using an installed professional report/document-generation capability where available. Render with restrained professional assurance styling:

- A4/Letter friendly margins and page breaks;
- neutral sans-serif body typography and strong heading hierarchy;
- cover page with application, assessment period, CASA version and confidentiality classification;
- repeating footer with report title/version and page number;
- compact KPI/result table, not decorative dashboard cards;
- finding callouts that emphasize rating, CASA criteria and status without excessive color;
- tables that repeat headers across pages;
- appendices for detailed matrices/evidence rather than overcrowding the executive section;
- no third-party professional-services logos, trademarks or visual imitation that implies affiliation.

The visual quality may resemble a mature external assurance report, but branding must remain the project/developer's own.


## Cross-format consistency

Use the same frozen report content as the source for Markdown, DOCX and PDF. Before delivery:

- reconcile cover metadata, CASA version, application commit and dates;
- reconcile control-result and finding counts;
- confirm every finding appears in all three formats;
- confirm appendices are complete;
- inspect DOCX and PDF page breaks, repeating table headers, footers and page numbers;
- verify that long URLs, code references and evidence IDs do not clip or overflow;
- record output hashes in the rendering manifest.

## Disclaimer treatment

The full disclaimer must be clearly readable and must not be visually minimized. Use normal body text or a dedicated boxed notice, not tiny legal text.

The footer short form should read substantially:

`Independent CASA Readiness Assessment | Not Certification`

Do not place Google, App Defense Alliance, Big Four, laboratory or other third-party logos on the report unless the executing organization has an independent legal right to do so.
